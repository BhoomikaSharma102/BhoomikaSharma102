<h3>📈 Renewal Forecasting & Recurring Revenue Lifecycle</h3>

<p>
<strong>Salesforce Technical Lead</strong>
</p>

<p>
Designed and implemented an isolated renewal forecasting component
that separates <strong>retained recurring revenue</strong> from
<strong>at-risk/churn revenue</strong>. The architecture supports
open and closed renewal states, automatically transitions forecasts
to actual outcomes, clears stale projections, and handles
multi-currency dated conversion.
</p>

<p>
The solution was designed to coexist with <strong>9+ years of
legacy Opportunity automation</strong> without requiring a rewrite,
while minimizing regression risk through selective recalculation
and an isolated processing boundary.
</p>

<p>
<strong>Key Architecture:</strong>
Apex • Opportunity Lifecycle • ARR • Sales Cloud •
Multi-Currency • Integration-Aware Processing • Unit Testing •
Regression Testing • Root Cause Analysis
</p>

<p>
<strong>Focus:</strong> Revenue Visibility • Forecasting •
Churn Detection • Maintainability • Legacy Automation Integration
</p>

<h2>🏗️ Architecture Principles</h2>

<table>
  <tr>
    <td><strong>Standard Before Custom</strong></td>
    <td>
      Use Salesforce capabilities first and introduce custom
      development where business complexity requires it.
    </td>
  </tr>

  <tr>
    <td><strong>Configuration Over Hardcoding</strong></td>
    <td>
      Design solutions that can evolve with changing business
      requirements without unnecessary deployments.
    </td>
  </tr>

  <tr>
    <td><strong>Service-Layer Architecture</strong></td>
    <td>
      Keep complex business logic reusable and decoupled from
      triggers and user interfaces.
    </td>
  </tr>

  <tr>
    <td><strong>Bulk-Safe by Design</strong></td>
    <td>
      Build with Salesforce governor limits and multi-record
      execution in mind from the beginning.
    </td>
  </tr>

  <tr>
    <td><strong>Security on the Platform</strong></td>
    <td>
      Configuration controls presentation while Salesforce
      controls data access and security.
    </td>
  </tr>

  <tr>
    <td><strong>Data Integrity First</strong></td>
    <td>
      Prioritize validation, reconciliation, traceability and
      relationship integrity.
    </td>
  </tr>

  <tr>
    <td><strong>Design for Change</strong></td>
    <td>
      Architect solutions for the tenth business change,
      not just the first release.
    </td>
  </tr>
</table>
